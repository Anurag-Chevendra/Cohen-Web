THE MOST IMPORTANT CLAUSE

A verdict is evidence, not a judgement

Cohen reports what a package did when it ran in a sandbox. That is all it reports. A review verdict means the sandbox observed behaviour a person should look at, and it is never an assertion that a package or its author is malicious. A clean verdict means nothing correlated on the run we did, at the time we did it, and is not a guarantee that a package is safe.

You are responsible for what you do with a verdict. Do not treat Cohen as the sole basis for blocking a deployment, removing a package, or making any accusation about a person or organisation. Fred Cohen proved in 1984 that the general problem is undecidable; we do not claim to have solved it.
