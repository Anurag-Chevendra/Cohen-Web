QUESTIONS

Before you pick one

What counts as a deep analysis?

One package version, run once. Results are cached across every customer, so you only spend one on a version nobody has run yet.

What happens if I run out?

Individual stops at the limit. Team keeps going at $0.02 an analysis and tells you first. Nothing upgrades without asking.

Does a scanner plan include Observability?

No. They are separate products with separate meters, and either can be bought alone.

Do I pay for Mantis?

No. It is free and open source on every plan, Free included.
